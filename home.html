About Pollachi

Pollachi is a beautiful town in Coimbatore district, Tamil Nadu. It is known for its lush green landscapes, coconut plantations, agriculture, and pleasant climate. Surrounded by the Western Ghats, Pollachi offers a perfect blend of nature, culture, and modern lifestyle. The town is also famous for its markets, temples, educational institutions and friendly people.
